# Office & Workplace Hygiene Resources for Perth Workplaces

This repository compiles practical, research-backed cleaning guidelines for businesses in Perth.  
The goal is to help workplaces reduce illness, improve indoor air quality, and maintain professional environments.

### Included Resources
- Daily and weekly office cleaning checklists  
- High-touch surface sanitisation guidelines  
- Carpet and upholstery maintenance tips  
- Waste separation & recycling practices  
- Eco-friendly cleaning recommendations  
- School, clinic, and industrial facility considerations  

These guidelines align with the cleaning standards used by professional cleaning teams in Perth.
