# Workplace Hygiene & Cleaning Standards for Perth Businesses

This repository compiles practical, research-backed guidance to help workplaces in Perth maintain clean, healthy, and productive environments. These resources are drawn from real-world experience in commercial offices, medical clinics, educational facilities, and industrial buildings.

Our goal is to make workplace hygiene knowledge accessible to everyone — whether you're a small business owner, school administrator, or facility manager.

---

## 🧼 1. Daily Office Hygiene Checklist

- Disinfect high-touch points: door handles, shared desks, elevator buttons  
- Wipe down keyboards, mice, and desk surfaces  
- Empty waste bins & recycling  
- Clean shared kitchen surfaces  
- Spot-clean visible spills and stains  
- Ensure ventilation and airflow

## 🌿 3. Eco-Friendly Cleaning in Workplaces

Environmentally safe cleaning products reduce indoor chemical exposure, protect sensitive surfaces, and improve air quality.

Recommended practices:

- Use biodegradable disinfectants  
- Avoid chlorine-based products in enclosed areas  
- Choose reusable microfiber cloths  
- Reduce aerosol cleaners  
- Follow manufacturer guidelines for upholstery and carpets

---

## 🧽 4. Carpet & Upholstery Maintenance

Carpets trap dust, pollen, and microorganisms.  
Upholstery absorbs moisture and odors.

Maintenance recommendations:

- Vacuum carpets daily in high-traffic zones  
- Schedule deep cleaning every 3–6 months  
- Steam clean chairs and sofas periodically  
- Rotate heavy furniture to reduce wear patterns
